1. Get some (6-sided) dice, preferably casino rated. The more you have the quicker it will go
2. Print one page of BitsToWords.pdf (which one depends on if you want 12 or 24 words. 12 is sufficient)
   (Fill the Identifier with a name so you later know which seed this is)
   Each numbered bigger rectangle will represent be a word.
   The word will be noted in the lower wider rectangle.
   The smaller 11 reectangles above, are the "bits" (each is either a "0" or a "1" at the end)
   We will first fill the bits using the dice and then derive the words from the bits.
3. Print BIP39_Wordlist_Binary_Decimal_Searchable.pdf
   This will help us derive the words from the bits.
   Important: Do print this out. Do NOT search in this file on your desktop for the bits you have rolled. Even partially. 
4. Get into a room w/o cameras or phones nearby

5. Roll all dice. Bring them into a line
   (important: do not look at them during this procedure or order them. Do this always in the same way for each consecutive roll to not screw up your entropy)
6. From left to right for each dice:
   If it is a 1,2 or 3: enter a "0" into the next free small rectangle (bit)
   If it is a 4,5 or 6: enter a "1" into the next free small rectangle (bit)
Repeat 5. & 6. until you have filled all small rectangles/bits except the ones behind the last bold "|" of the last word
(So for the 12 seed sheet this means all but the last 4, for the 24 seed sheet this is all but the last 8)

7. For each word, look for the word that has the same 11 bits as you rolled in your printout of BIP39_Wordlist_Binary_Decimal_Searchable.pdf
   The first three bits of the word tell you which page you need to look into.
   The next three bits of the word tell you which column of that page you need to look into.
   The last five bits of the word tell you which row of that page you need to look into.
8. For the last word there are still some bits missing (4 bits for 12 words, 8 bits for 24 words).
   To find them you will need the help of a hardware wallet.
   (because they are checksum, i.e. they are fully determined by the other bits. The computation is quite complex)
   The existing bits you have rolled limit the options to 16 (i.e. half a column for 12 words) / 256 (one page for 24 words) words.
   You probably do not want to try all of these manually.
   How to do this will depend on your concrete HW wallet.
   Usually you enter the first eleven words as you normally would when entering an existing seedphrase and then the hw wallet will propose possible last words that lead to a valid checksum.
   Choose one of those that is in the list of options you determined.
