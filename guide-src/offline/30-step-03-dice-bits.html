Step 3 · roll and look up

Roll your batch, then enter each result.

Roll as many dice per throw as you want.

  1. 1 Put all chosen dice in the lidded box. Shake hard at least ten times so dice travel and collide audibly, then gently tip settled dice to one edge.
  2. 2 Open without touching dice by hand. Read left to right, (if need be: front to back), using the same fixed reading order for every throw.
  3. 3 For each die: select the face below (or press 1–6) in that order. Bits fill in automatically and each word appears as soon as its 11 bits are complete.
  1. 1 Shake dice in the cup, then cast them with enough room and energy for several impacts on the hard tray or board.
  2. 2 Read every die from left to right (and if need be: front to back), using the same fixed reading order for every throw.
  3. 3 For each die: select the face below (or press 1–6) in that order. Bits fill in automatically and each word appears as soon as its 11 bits are complete.

Unclear result? If a die is cocked or ambiguous, discard the whole batch and re-roll the complete batch.

Repeat steps 1–3 until all required bits are filled. Bits that would cross the checksum boundary are discarded automatically.
