Dice-to-bit method

Base-4 rejection sampling gives two bits from each accepted roll. Faces 5 and 6 are ignored.

You will collect 64 accepted results. Expect about 96 physical rolls on average.

Binary quantization uses every result: faces 1–3 mean 0, and faces 4–6 mean 1.

You will make exactly 128 rolls. Use this only with casino-grade dice, or select 24 words for more margin.

Choose another combination. Binary quantization with consumer-grade dice requires the 24-word option. Select 24 words or use base-4.
